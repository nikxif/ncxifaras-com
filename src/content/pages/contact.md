The most reliable way to reach me is email:

**[info@ncxifaras.com](mailto:info@ncxifaras.com)**

There's no contact form here on purpose to avoid third-party data processors. Email goes straight to me and nowhere else.

## What I'm glad to hear about

- **Corrections**. If something here is wrong, I want to know; especially on the policy and clinical posts.
- **Questions** about anything I've written, including the technical posts.
- **Collaboration** on health-policy, digital-health, software, or research work (or other topics, feel free to suggest).
- **Consulting** and advisory enquiries: I take on remote, part-time work alongside regular practice.

## What I'll probably not reply to

- Clinical questions about your own health. I can't give medical advice to someone who isn't my patient, and it would be irresponsible to try. Please speak to your own doctor.
- SEO, link-building, and guest-post offers.
- Anything sent to several dozen people at once.
